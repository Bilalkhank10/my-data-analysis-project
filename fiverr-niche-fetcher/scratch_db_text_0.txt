FAQ

What platforms do you work with for dashboard integration?

I work with Google Analytics, Google Ads, Facebook Ads, Instagram, Google Sheets, BigQuery, SQL, Chargebee, and more. Let me know your data source, and I’ll confirm if I can connect it.

Can you match the design of my website or brand in the dashboard?

Yes! I can fully customize the dashboard’s visual style (colors, fonts, logos) to match your branding and website design.

Do you fix issues like broken filters or wrong data in reports?

Absolutely. I specialize in fixing blended data, formulas, broken filters, and misconfigured metrics in Looker Studio or Google Data Studio.

What if I need help after delivery?

I offer unlimited revisions and post-delivery support. My goal is to make sure your dashboard works perfectly and meets your expectations.
